<h2><a id="features"></a>Features</h2>

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

#### Playback & Controls
- Stream high-quality audio tracks & video playback
- Full background playback & offline track caching
- Automatic silence skipping & customizable sleep timer
- Built-in equalizer, pitch shifting & tempo adjustment

</td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

#### Library & Management
- Comprehensive playlist management (Local & Imported)
- Synchronize saved artists, albums, and playlists
- Live synchronized lyrics with AI-assisted translation
- Deep search engine across songs, artists, and albums

</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

#### Social Features
- Real-time *Listen Together* session support

</td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

#### Visual Interface
- Complete user interface polish & streamlined layouts
- Home screen widget support
- Light, Dark, Pure Black (OLED), and Material 3 Dynamic themes

</td>
  </tr>
</table>
